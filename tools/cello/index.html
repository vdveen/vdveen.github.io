Play the line on your cello. Take your time: it waits for each note.

Allow the microphone when the browser asks. Play in a quiet room, with the phone on your music stand. If the app keeps hearing the wrong note, move the phone a bit closer.

Drone

A drone is a long held note. Play your notes against it and listen: when you are in tune, the sound gets calm and the wobble stops. Use headphones if you want the tuner on at the same time.
