Small web apps I built for my own days. Most of them do one thing for one person, which is the point.

  • Still

    A meditation timer with four bells, a countdown or open-ended mode, and a tap that turns the screen black. Finished sessions can go to Apple Health through a Shortcut.

  • Cello notes

    Learning to read notes for Cello Time Joggers. Flash cards, a play-along that listens through the microphone, and a tuner.

  • World Atlas

    A live world clock map for a wall screen: a flat map and a 3D globe with day and night, earthquakes, storms, satellites, clouds and space weather, updated all day.

  • Geochron 3D

    A live globe with day and night, clouds, the ISS, bright satellites, earthquakes and aurora. The flat 2D version adds time zones, the analemma and your own satellites from a TLE file.

  • Commute planner

    A Swiss station clock that tells me which bus to take right now and how long until it leaves.

  • Morning routine

    Pick how much slack you want, how you travel and how long breakfast takes, and it tells you when to set the alarm.

  • Banenzwemmen Amerena

    When the next lane-swimming session is at the Amerena pool in Amersfoort, and whether you get the whole pool or a few 50-metre lanes.

  • Income split

    Splits a household bill in proportion to two incomes and shows the sum step by step.

  • The Daily

    A one-page morning newspaper, made to read on an e-ink screen.

  • Treintijden

    NS departure times for my TRMNL e-ink display, and a phone notification that shows whether my trains are on time.
